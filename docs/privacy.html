Privacy Policy

Interval Studio – interval training timer

Effective 23 October 2026

In short: Interval Studio has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Your workouts, settings and voice recordings stay on your phone. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.

Who we are

Interval Studio is made by Pete Jackson, an independent developer. For any question about this policy or your data, email interval.studio.app@gmail.com.

What the app stores, and where

  • Your workouts and settings – the workouts you build and your sound and screen choices. They are saved in the app’s own storage on your phone.
  • Voice recordings – only if you use “My voice” to record your own countdown and call-outs. The recordings are saved in the app’s own storage on your phone and used only to play your cues during a workout.

We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, health or fitness data, or any identifier for your device. The app does not include advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Microphone – used only while you record your own voice cues, and only after you tap the record button. Nothing is recorded at any other time, and recordings never leave your phone.
  • Notifications – used to show the running workout on your lock screen and in the notification shade, with pause and skip buttons.
  • Keep the phone awake / run in the background – used so the timer and its sounds keep going while the screen is off. Nothing is stored or sent because of this.

Who your data is shared with

No one. Interval Studio does not send your data to us or to any third party. The only time data leaves the app is when you choose to:

  • Export your library – a backup file of your workouts (not your voice recordings) is saved or sent wherever you pick, such as your Downloads folder, Google Drive, email or a messaging app. From then on, that copy is handled by the service you chose, under its own privacy policy.
  • Android’s own backup – if backup is turned on in your phone’s settings, Android may include the app’s data in your Google account backup, so it can be restored on a new phone. This is controlled by your phone settings and Google, not by Interval Studio.

Keeping your data safe

Your data is kept in the app’s private storage on your phone, which other apps cannot read. Because nothing is sent over the internet, there is nothing on our side that could be lost or breached.

How long data is kept, and how to delete it

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it. You can:

  • delete a workout from its ⋯ menu,
  • delete your voice recordings in Settings → Voice → My voice → Delete recordings,
  • delete everything by clearing the app’s storage in your phone’s settings, or by uninstalling the app.

We hold no copy of your data, so there is nothing for us to delete. Backup files you exported are yours to keep or delete.

Children

Interval Studio is a general fitness tool and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts handling data differently, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change.
